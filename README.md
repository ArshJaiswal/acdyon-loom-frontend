# ✦ LOOM — Wear What Feels Like You

> **Acdyon Technologies Frontend Challenge — Part 2: The Premium Home Page**  
> *A concept landing page prototype for a curated everyday fashion brand.*  
> **Author**: Arsh Jaiswal  

---

## 🌟 Overview

**LOOM** is a concept home page for a modern, minimal everyday fashion platform. The brand concept centers on curated wardrobe essentials—breathable linen, organic cotton jersey, and relaxed silhouettes designed for everyday wear.

Built with **pure modern Vanilla Web standards (HTML5, CSS3, ES6+)**, this prototype focuses on visual craft, editorial typography, generous whitespace, restrained micro-interactions, and real dark mode support.

---

## ✨ Page Structure & Key Highlights

1. **Top Announcement Bar**:
   - `Collection 01 — Limited Seasonal Release`

2. **Navigation Bar**:
   - Clean editorial branding (`LOOM`), links (`New Arrivals`, `Collection`, `Lookbook`, `Philosophy`), Theme toggle (Light/Dark), Search, and Bag drawer icon.

3. **Hero Section**:
   - Category tag: `COLLECTION 01`
   - Headline: *"Wear what feels like you."*
   - Subtitle: *"Curated essentials designed for everyday confidence. Relaxed silhouettes cut from breathable linen and organic cotton."*
   - Direct CTA: `Explore Collection →`
   - Large editorial image with floating garment tag.

4. **The Everyday Wardrobe (Interactive Product Showcase)**:
   - 4 core product cards:
     - Alternate angle image swap on card hover.
     - Product title, fabric description, and price in INR (₹2,499).
     - **Interactive Color Swatches (`○ ○ ○`)**: Clicking any color swatch instantly updates the product image and active outline in real time.
     - `+ Quick Add` action on hover.

5. **The Loom Edit (Lookbook)**:
   - Editorial asymmetric magazine grid showcasing relaxed silhouettes (Look 01, Look 02, Look 03).

6. **The Loom Standard (Philosophy)**:
   - 3 clean pillars:
     - `01` **Natural Materials**: Breathable linen and organic cotton selected for everyday wear.
     - `02` **Considered Construction**: Relaxed silhouettes with details designed to last.
     - `03` **Less, Better**: Small seasonal releases instead of endless drops.

7. **Final Call to Action**:
   - *"Find your next favorite piece."*
   - `Explore Collection →`

8. **Slide-Over Bag Drawer**:
   - Focused, clean demo interaction displaying added items, quantity adjustments (+/-), and subtotal calculation.

9. **Complete Dark Mode**:
   - Cohesive monochrome dark palette with automatic OS preference detection, persistent local storage, and instant toggle.

10. **Accessibility & Responsive Polish**:
    - `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
    - Verified at **390px mobile** (iPhone) to **1440px+ desktop** with zero horizontal overflow.
    - Visible focus styling and keyboard shortcuts (<kbd>/</kbd>, <kbd>Esc</kbd>).

11. **🎮 Secret Easter Egg (Konami Code)**:
    - Type `↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A` to unlock a subtle **Atelier Mode** banner.

12. **📄 1-Page `DECISIONS.md`**:
    - Grounded explanation answering why this approach was chosen, trade-offs made, and verification details.
