# ✦ LOOM — Wear What Feels Like You

> **Acdyon Technologies Frontend Challenge — Part 2: The Premium Home Page**  
> *"Build It Like You Mean It"*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**LOOM** is a modern, minimal, premium fashion shopping platform. It produces curated everyday fashion essentials designed for effortless confidence—cut from breathable linen and organic cotton with relaxed silhouettes.

Built with **pure modern Vanilla Web standards (HTML5, CSS3, ES6+)**, LOOM focuses on visual craft, editorial typography, generous whitespace, and restrained micro-interactions.

---

## ✨ Page Structure & Key Highlights

1. **Top Announcement Bar**:
   - `Collection 01 — Limited Seasonal Release`

2. **Navigation Bar**:
   - Clean editorial branding (`LOOM`), links (`New Arrivals`, `Collection`, `Lookbook`, `Philosophy`), keyboard-accessible Search (`/`), and Bag icon.

3. **Hero Section (First 3-Second "WOW" Impression)**:
   - Category tag: `COLLECTION 01`
   - Headline: *"Wear what feels like you."*
   - Subtitle: *"Curated essentials designed for everyday confidence. Relaxed silhouettes cut from breathable linen and organic cotton."*
   - Direct CTA: `Explore Collection →`
   - Large editorial image with floating product tag.

4. **The Everyday Wardrobe (Interactive Product Showcase)**:
   - 4 core product cards matching the exact layout:
     - Alternate angle image swap on card hover.
     - Product title & fabric description.
     - Price in INR (₹2,499).
     - **Interactive Color Swatches (`○ ○ ○`)**: Clicking any color swatch instantly updates the product image and active outline in real time.
     - Quick "Add to Bag" action on hover.

5. **The Loom Edit (Lookbook)**:
   - Editorial asymmetric magazine grid showcasing real-life silhouettes (Look 01, Look 02, Look 03).

6. **The Loom Standard (Philosophy)**:
   - 3 clean pillars:
     - `01` **Natural Materials**: Lightweight linen and organic cotton selected for everyday wear.
     - `02` **Considered Construction**: Relaxed silhouettes with details designed to last.
     - `03` **Less, Better**: Small seasonal releases instead of endless drops.

7. **Final Call to Action**:
   - *"Find your next favorite piece."*
   - `Explore Collection →`

8. **Slide-Over Bag Drawer**:
   - Focused, clean demo interaction showing added items, quantity adjustments (+/-), and subtotal calculation.

9. **Accessibility & Responsive Polish**:
   - `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
   - Tested for **390px mobile** (iPhone) to **1440px+ desktop** with zero horizontal overflow.
   - Visible `:focus-visible` styling and keyboard accessibility (<kbd>/</kbd>, <kbd>Esc</kbd>).

10. **🎮 Secret Easter Egg (Konami Code)**:
    - Type `↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A` to unlock a subtle **Atelier Mode** banner.

11. **📄 1-Page `DECISIONS.md`**:
    - Comprehensive writeup answering all three required challenge questions.
